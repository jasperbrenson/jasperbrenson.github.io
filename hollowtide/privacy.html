English

Controller: JasperBrenson, developer of Hollowtide. Contact: noleadgeneration@gmail.com

Data we process

  • Game saves (progress, settings) stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Purchases: the "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.
  • Consent (EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch we ask for consent with Google's form. You can change it at any time from the game settings. Without consent ads are not personalised.
  • Children: the app is not directed at children under 13; minors only see non-personalised ads.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts or photos.

Your rights

You can request information, access or deletion by writing to the address above. Uninstalling the app removes the local game data. For data processed by Google you can also use My Ad Center.
